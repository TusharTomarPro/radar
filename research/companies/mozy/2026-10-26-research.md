# Mozy

Researched: 2026-10-26

**Category:** app-blocker

**What it does:** Mozy locks selected apps on Android and iPhone until the user walks a predefined number of steps, encouraging physical activity by tying phone usage to movement.

**Likely revenue model:** Freemium model with a subscription tier that unlocks unlimited app blocks, custom step goals, and advanced analytics.

**India equivalent already exists:** none found

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The concept relies on widespread use of smartphones with built-in step counters and a cultural willingness to link app usage to physical activity, which may be less prevalent in India where many users lack consistent access to health-tracking wearables.
