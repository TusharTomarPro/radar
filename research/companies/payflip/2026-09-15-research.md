# Payflip

Researched: 2026-09-15

**Category:** p2p-payments

**What it does:** Payflip lets users send money to anyone by simply entering a name, eliminating the need for IBANs, wallet addresses, or other account details. It creates a name‑to‑account mapping behind the scenes to route the funds.

**Likely revenue model:** The service likely charges a small transaction fee or a percentage markup on each transfer, possibly with premium features behind a subscription.

**India equivalent already exists:** PhonePe

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** India already has widespread UPI adoption where payments are triggered via phone numbers or UPI IDs; a name‑based system would need a central name directory and user trust, which may be harder to establish but could piggyback on existing UPI infrastructure.
