# Trial Tracker

Researched: 2026-09-06

**Category:** subscription-management

**What it does:** A lightweight tool that lets users record and monitor the start and end dates of free trials to avoid unwanted charges. It sends reminders before trials expire.

**Likely revenue model:** Freemium model with a paid tier for premium features like multi-device sync, advanced reminders, and analytics.

**India equivalent already exists:** Walnut

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The concept relies on users regularly managing multiple digital subscriptions, which is growing in India but still limited to tech‑savvy urban users; integration with UPI and local payment reminders would be needed.
