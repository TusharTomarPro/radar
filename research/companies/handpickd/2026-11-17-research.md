# Handpickd

Researched: 2026-11-17

**Category:** agri-commerce

**What it does:** Handpickd runs an inventory‑free farm‑to‑fresh delivery service that collects customer orders, sources the exact quantities from farmers, and delivers the produce the next morning. It aims to minimise waste by avoiding warehousing and dark‑store stock.

**Likely revenue model:** The company earns revenue by selling farm produce directly to consumers at a markup and also pockets interest income from customer deposits.

**India equivalent already exists:** Milkbasket

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The model relies on a highly responsive supply chain, reliable cold‑chain logistics and consumers willing to pay a premium for next‑day fresh produce—capabilities that are uneven across Indian tier‑2 and tier‑3 cities. Scaling such on‑demand procurement also demands strong farmer onboarding platforms that are still nascent in many regions.
