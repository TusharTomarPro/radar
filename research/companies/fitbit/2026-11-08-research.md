# Fitbit

Researched: 2026-11-08

**Category:** wearables

**What it does:** The Fitbit Air is a screen‑less fitness tracker that can be clipped onto the strap of any traditional analog watch, turning it into a smart‑trackable accessory. It monitors activity, heart rate, and sleep while relying on the watch’s existing design.

**Likely revenue model:** Revenue comes primarily from one‑time hardware sales of the Fitbit Air, supplemented by optional subscription services for advanced health insights.

**India equivalent already exists:** none found

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The concept relies on a sizable market of analog watch owners willing to modify their straps, which is less common in India where consumers tend to favor integrated smartwatches or low‑cost fitness bands. Additionally, Bluetooth pairing and app ecosystem adoption may be slower in regions with limited data connectivity.
