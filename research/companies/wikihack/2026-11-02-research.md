# Wikihack

Researched: 2026-11-02

**Category:** content-aggregator

**What it does:** Wikihack scrapes Hacker News for any links or mentions of Wikipedia articles, ranks them and presents a scrollable feed of the most discussed pages. Users can browse top, random, or rising Wikipedia articles based on HN activity.

**Likely revenue model:** Probably ad‑supported or donation‑based, as the service is free and front‑end only.

**India equivalent already exists:** none found

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The product relies on a sizable Hacker News user base, which is relatively small in India, limiting local traction. Technically it works anywhere with internet access, but the audience behavior may not exist at scale in India.
