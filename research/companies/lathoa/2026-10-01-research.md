# Lathoa

Researched: 2026-10-01

**Category:** edtech

**What it does:** Lathoa is a math learning app for kids aged 10‑14 where an AI tutor deliberately makes a mistake in a step‑by‑step solution, and the child must identify and explain the error to earn points. The app uses multiple models and arithmetic checks to ensure the intentional error is valid before showing it to the user.
