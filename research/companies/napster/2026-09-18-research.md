# Napster

Researched: 2026-09-18

**Category:** edtech

**What it does:** Napster is building AI-driven digital clones of teachers that can deliver personalized lessons, answer student queries, and grade assignments. The platform aims to replicate a teacher's style and knowledge for on‑demand tutoring.

**Likely revenue model:** It will likely charge schools or individual learners a subscription or licensing fee for access to the AI teacher avatars.

**India equivalent already exists:** Byju's

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** India has strong mobile internet penetration and a huge demand for scalable tutoring, but widespread adoption may be limited by varying broadband quality in rural areas and cultural preferences for human teachers.
