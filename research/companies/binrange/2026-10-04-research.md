# BinRange

Researched: 2026-10-04

**Category:** waste-tech

**What it does:** BinRange equips municipal waste bins with ultra‑wideband (UWB) radios that broadcast precise real‑time location data, allowing cities to optimise collection routes and reduce missed pickups.

**Likely revenue model:** They sell the UWB sensor hardware to municipalities and charge a recurring subscription for data analytics, routing software, and ongoing support.

**India equivalent already exists:** SmartBin (SmartBin Technologies)

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The solution relies on high‑precision UWB positioning and a centralized data platform, which may be costly and require municipalities to adopt formal route‑planning processes that are less common in India's largely informal waste‑collection ecosystem.
