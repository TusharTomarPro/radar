# Eazeebox

Researched: 2026-11-20

**Category:** B2B marketplace

**What it does:** Offers a single mobile app that lets neighbourhood electrical retailers order stock from multiple brands, simplifying restocking and inventory management.

**Likely revenue model:** Earns revenue by taking a commission or transaction fee on each order placed through the platform, and may also charge subscription fees to retailers for premium features.
