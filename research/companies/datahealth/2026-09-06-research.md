# DataHealth

Researched: 2026-09-06

**Category:** data-quality

**What it does:** Monitors revenue and customer data for mismatches, missing fields, and duplicates, then emails a weekly health report to the user’s Gmail. It surfaces the most common data problems so they can be fixed before AI or analytics are applied.

**Likely revenue model:** A subscription‑based SaaS model, likely tiered by the number of records or integrations.
