# Henfruit

Researched: 2026-11-18

**Category:** food-tech

**What it does:** Henfruit sells branded, traceable eggs that are tested daily for quality and safety, offering consumers access to lab reports online. It operates automated cleaning, sanitisation and grading facilities to ensure consistent premium product.

**Likely revenue model:** Revenue comes from premium pricing of branded eggs sold through online and offline retail channels, possibly with subscription or bulk‑order options for households and businesses.
