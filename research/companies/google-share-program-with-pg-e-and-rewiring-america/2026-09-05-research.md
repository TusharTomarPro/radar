# Google SHARE Program (with PG&E and Rewiring America)

Researched: 2026-09-05

**Category:** energy-tech

**What it does:** Google is partnering with utility PG&E and nonprofit Rewiring America to help Bay Area homeowners buy heat pumps and home batteries at steep discounts, while pooling those devices into a virtual power plant that discharges stored energy back to the grid during peak demand.

**Likely revenue model:** Google likely funds customer incentives as a capital expenditure to secure flexible grid capacity for its data centers at a lower cost than building new power plants or transmission lines.

**India equivalent already exists:** Tata Power and Sunrun-style aggregators like Amp Energy India (virtual power plant pilots)

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** Heat pump adoption in India is still nascent and largely limited to premium housing due to high upfront costs and lack of awareness, while rooftop solar-plus-battery penetration is low outside metros. Virtual power plant programs also depend on advanced smart-metering infrastructure and utility regulation (e.g., net metering and grid-scale dispatch rules) that exist only in pockets in India.
