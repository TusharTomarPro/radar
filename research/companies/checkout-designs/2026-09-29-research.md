# Checkout Designs

Researched: 2026-09-29

**Category:** home-tech

**What it does:** Checkout Designs is a digital platform that lets homeowners visualize, select, and order interior finishes such as marble, tiles, and fixtures, streamlining the renovation decision‑making process.
