# TruthScore

Researched: 2026-09-06

**Category:** fact-checking

**What it does:** A Chrome extension that analyzes web search results and assigns a credibility score to each page, helping users gauge the truthfulness of information.

**Likely revenue model:** A freemium/subscription model where basic scoring is free and advanced features or higher query limits require a paid plan.

**India equivalent already exists:** Factly

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** Success relies on users regularly installing Chrome extensions and trusting algorithmic credibility scores, which may be slower to adopt in India where local fact‑checking portals and language‑specific content dominate.

**Badge guess:** orange
