# StateGuard

Researched: 2026-09-06

**Category:** dev-tools

**What it does:** StateGuard is a runtime library that automatically normalizes mismatched payloads from different providers, turning equivalent values into a consistent format to prevent crashes. It aims to repair semantic errors on the fly while allowing developers to opt‑in for strict failure modes.

**Likely revenue model:** Probably a freemium model: free open‑source core with paid enterprise licensing, support contracts, or SaaS monitoring dashboards.

**India equivalent already exists:** AppDynamics

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The solution relies on mature CI/CD pipelines, extensive logging, and a culture of automated error handling that is common in large Indian tech firms but may be less prevalent among early‑stage startups lacking robust DevOps practices.
