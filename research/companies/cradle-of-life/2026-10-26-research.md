# Cradle Of Life

Researched: 2026-10-26

**Category:** senior-living

**What it does:** Cradle Of Life builds and operates senior‑living communities in India that focus on lifestyle, social interaction and wellness rather than just housing. It designs spaces and services around how retirees want to live, not just where they sleep.

**Likely revenue model:** Revenue likely comes from a mix of rental or lease fees for residential units, membership/subscription charges for community services, and ancillary income from health, dining and activity programs.

**India equivalent already exists:** Senior Living India
