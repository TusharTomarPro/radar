# Onto

Researched: 2026-10-21

**Category:** travel-tech

**What it does:** Onto is a travel platform that lets users discover and book hotels and automatically refunds the price difference if a booked hotel’s rate drops later, even for bookings made on other sites.

**Likely revenue model:** It likely earns commissions on hotel bookings and may take a small fee or share of the refunded amount from partner hotels or aggregators.

**India equivalent already exists:** none found
