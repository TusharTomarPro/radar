**Reasoning:** The search evidence shows Sonos faces direct competition in India from established players like boAt (which has raised $171M and dominates the affordable segment), Bose, Sennheiser, and Bowers & Wilkins, plus emerging multi-room standards like WiiM and Bluesound. boAt's strong India presence with 55+ investors including Qualcomm Ventures signals a well-funded local competitor already owning the value segment.
