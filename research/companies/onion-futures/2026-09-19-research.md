# Onion Futures

Researched: 2026-09-19

**Category:** agri-tech

**What it does:** Provides a digital marketplace where growers, distributors and buyers can trade onion futures contracts to hedge price risk. The platform matches counterparties and settles contracts based on market prices.

**Likely revenue model:** Charges a commission or transaction fee on each futures contract traded, plus possible data or subscription fees for market analytics.

**India equivalent already exists:** NCDEX

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** India already trades onion futures on the National Commodity & Derivatives Exchange, so the regulatory and market infrastructure exists, but small farmers may lack digital access and trust in online platforms.
