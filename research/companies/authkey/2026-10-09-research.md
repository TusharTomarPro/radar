# Authkey

Researched: 2026-10-09

**Category:** conversational-ai

**What it does:** Authkey offers a unified platform that merges SMS, WhatsApp, RCS and AI to enable businesses to run automated, two‑way customer conversations instead of simple notifications.

**Likely revenue model:** Subscription‑based SaaS pricing with tiered plans based on message volume and AI usage, plus per‑message or per‑conversation fees.

**India equivalent already exists:** Gupshup

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** India already has massive WhatsApp and SMS usage, but RCS adoption is still low and AI‑driven chatbots require robust data‑privacy compliance and reliable internet connectivity, which may limit full rollout in tier‑2/3 markets.
