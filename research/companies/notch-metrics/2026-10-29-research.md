# Notch Metrics

Researched: 2026-10-29

**Category:** analytics

**What it does:** Provides real‑time revenue and product‑analytics dashboards that appear in the smartphone notch, letting founders and product teams glance at key metrics without opening an app.

**Likely revenue model:** A subscription‑based SaaS model with tiered plans based on data volume and feature access.

**India equivalent already exists:** MoEngage

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The concept relies on widespread use of notched smartphones and a culture of monitoring metrics on personal devices, which may be less common in India where many users still prefer desktop dashboards and where data‑privacy concerns could limit on‑screen metric displays.
