# Swish

Researched: 2026-09-10

**Category:** quick-commerce

**What it does:** Swish runs a network of neighbourhood kitchens that prepare fresh meals and deliver them to customers within about 10 minutes.

**Likely revenue model:** Earns revenue by taking a commission or markup on each food order placed through its platform.

**India equivalent already exists:** Swiggy Bolt

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The model relies on ultra‑dense kitchen clusters, high‑speed delivery fleets and consumers accustomed to ordering food instantly; heavy traffic, variable road conditions and lower order density outside metro areas could hinder replication in many Indian cities.
