# Subscriptrack

Researched: 2026-11-11

**Category:** subscription-management

**What it does:** Subscriptrack links to a user's email inbox, scans receipts and notifications, and aggregates all recurring subscriptions into a single dashboard.

**Likely revenue model:** A freemium model where basic tracking is free and advanced features (e.g., cancellation assistance, spend analytics, premium alerts) are offered via a paid subscription.

**India equivalent already exists:** Walnut

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The service relies on email receipt parsing and integration with global payment providers; Indian users often receive subscription alerts via SMS or app notifications rather than email, which could limit data completeness unless the app adds local integrations.
