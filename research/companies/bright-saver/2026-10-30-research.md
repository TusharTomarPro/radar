# Bright Saver

Researched: 2026-10-30

**Category:** solar-tech

**What it does:** Bright Saver sells plug‑in solar kits—small rooftop or balcony panels with an inverter that plug directly into a standard wall outlet and feed excess power back to the grid.

**Likely revenue model:** Revenue comes from direct hardware sales of the kits (single or dual‑panel bundles) and optional membership fees that give discounted pricing.

**India equivalent already exists:** Tata Power Solar (balcony solar kits)

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** India’s dense apartment blocks often lack dedicated outdoor outlets and the regulatory framework for small‑scale grid‑connected devices is still evolving, so adoption would require new outlet standards or net‑metering approvals similar to the US UL certification hurdle.
