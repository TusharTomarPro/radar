# LaunchMetrics

Researched: 2026-09-06

**Category:** launch-analytics

**What it does:** Provides a simple dashboard that tracks key metrics for product launch pages, such as click‑throughs, drop‑off reasons, visitor sources, return rates and qualitative feedback.

**Likely revenue model:** Subscription‑based SaaS with tiered plans (free tier for basic tracking, paid tiers for deeper analytics and team collaboration).

**India equivalent already exists:** Zoho Analytics

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The service relies on a community of indie developers and early‑stage startups that actively share launch pages; while the analytics themselves are universal, the network effect and culture of rapid public testing are less prevalent in India, which could limit early adoption.
