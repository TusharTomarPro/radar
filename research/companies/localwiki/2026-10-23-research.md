# LocalWiki

Researched: 2026-10-23

**Category:** hyperlocal

**What it does:** LocalWiki creates a Wikipedia‑style knowledge base limited to points of interest that are within a half‑hour walk from the user’s location. It aggregates community‑generated articles, photos and maps for neighborhoods, parks, shops and other local landmarks.

**Likely revenue model:** It likely monetises through local business sponsorships, premium listings and data licensing to tourism or city‑planning platforms.

**India equivalent already exists:** none found
