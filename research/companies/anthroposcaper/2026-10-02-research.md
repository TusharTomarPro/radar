# Anthroposcaper

Researched: 2026-10-02

**Category:** urban-tech

**What it does:** Anthroposcaper lets users tag a 2D floor plan or site map and automatically generates a detailed 3D model of the urban environment. It streamlines the conversion from flat drawings to immersive 3‑D visualizations.

**Likely revenue model:** A SaaS subscription with tiered pricing based on the number of models generated or API calls, possibly supplemented by per‑project licensing for enterprise customers.

**India equivalent already exists:** MapmyIndia
