# Alter

Researched: 2026-10-21

**Category:** auth

**What it does:** Alter provides a proxy layer that injects per‑user credentials and enforces fine‑grained runtime policies for multi‑user AI or automation agents, recording audit logs for compliance. It lets developers build agents that act on behalf of individual users while keeping downstream systems secure.

**Likely revenue model:** Usage‑based pricing with a free tier up to 10k requests and paid tiers beyond that.

**India equivalent already exists:** LoginRadius

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The product assumes widespread internal use of chat‑based agents (e.g., Slack) and tight integration with SaaS APIs, which are common in large Indian enterprises but may be slower to adopt in smaller firms. Additionally, the compliance‑driven audit logging aligns well with Indian data‑privacy regulations, but requires mature DevSecOps practices that are still emerging in many Indian tech teams.

**Badge guess:** orange
