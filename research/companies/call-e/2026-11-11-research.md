# CALL-E

Researched: 2026-11-11

**Category:** voice-ai

**What it does:** CALL-E provides a phone number that routes inbound calls to AI agents, enabling voice‑based interactions with chatbots or virtual assistants. It handles telephony infrastructure, speech‑to‑text, and response generation so developers can add phone‑call capabilities without building the stack themselves.

**Likely revenue model:** Subscription or usage‑based pricing per phone number and per minute of call traffic, possibly with tiered plans for higher call volumes.

**India equivalent already exists:** Knowlarity

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** India has robust telecom infrastructure and a mature voice‑API market, but strict DND and consent regulations for automated calls could limit mass outreach. Adoption depends on businesses being comfortable with AI‑driven voice interactions and navigating RBI/telecom compliance.

**Badge guess:** orange
