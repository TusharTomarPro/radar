# WinterWear

Researched: 2026-10-13

**Category:** fashion-rental

**What it does:** WinterWear rents out seasonal cold‑weather clothing such as coats, jackets, sweaters and accessories on a short‑term basis, letting users swap pieces as the weather changes.
