# Triwarden

Researched: 2026-10-19

**Category:** password-manager

**What it does:** Triwarden offers a native macOS client that syncs with Bitwarden and its self‑hosted variant Vaultwarden, providing password storage, autofill, and secure notes on Mac devices.

**Likely revenue model:** A freemium model with a one‑time purchase or subscription for premium features such as advanced syncing, biometric unlock, and priority support.

**India equivalent already exists:** Zoho Vault
