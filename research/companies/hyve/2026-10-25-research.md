# Hyve

Researched: 2026-10-25

**Category:** parcel-locker

**What it does:** Hyve offers a smart lockbox that can be placed on a porch to securely receive packages, allowing delivery drivers to deposit gifts when there is space inside.

**Likely revenue model:** Sells the hardware lockbox directly to consumers and may charge a subscription for premium software features like real‑time alerts and remote access.

**India equivalent already exists:** Delhivery Smart Locker

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** India has growing e‑commerce volume and some locker deployments, but many deliveries are to apartments or gated societies where porch access is limited, and drivers may not be accustomed to using third‑party lockboxes, which could hinder adoption.
