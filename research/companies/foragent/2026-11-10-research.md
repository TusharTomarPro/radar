# ForAgent

Researched: 2026-11-10

**Category:** ai-agent

**What it does:** ForAgent supplies private phone numbers that AI agents can use to place and receive voice calls, enabling direct phone‑based conversations between bots. It abstracts telephony handling so developers can focus on the AI logic.

**Likely revenue model:** Subscription or usage‑based pricing per virtual number and per minute of call traffic.

**India equivalent already exists:** Exotel

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The model relies on robust telecom APIs and regulatory clearance for AI‑initiated calls, which exist in India but may face stricter consent and spam‑prevention rules, requiring additional compliance layers.
