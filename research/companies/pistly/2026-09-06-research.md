# Pistly

Researched: 2026-09-06

**Category:** sales-automation

**What it does:** Pistly is a lightweight CRM and email outreach tool that lets users verify emails, send personalized cold emails, and automate follow‑ups with CSV/PDF export. It offers a free tier plus Pro and Business subscription plans.

**Likely revenue model:** Subscription‑based SaaS with tiered pricing (free, Pro, Business) and possibly add‑on features.
