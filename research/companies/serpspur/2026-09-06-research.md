# Serpspur

Researched: 2026-09-06

**Category:** seo-tool

**What it does:** Provides a backlink gap analysis that compares a website against competitors and lists domains linking to rivals but not to the user. It helps SaaS owners discover missed backlink opportunities for outreach.

**Likely revenue model:** Offers a free basic version with paid subscription tiers for higher query limits, advanced data, and API access.

**India equivalent already exists:** RankWatch

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The model relies on a robust ecosystem of niche directories and resource pages, which exist in India, but outreach success may be limited by local language preferences and higher spam filter sensitivity. Infrastructure for data crawling is available, but cultural differences in link building could affect adoption.
