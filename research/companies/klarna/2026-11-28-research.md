# Klarna

Researched: 2026-11-28

**Category:** bnpl

**What it does:** Provides a 'buy now, pay later' checkout solution that lets shoppers split purchases into interest‑free installments or pay later.

**Likely revenue model:** Charges merchants a transaction fee and earns interest or late‑payment fees from consumers who opt for financed installments.

**India equivalent already exists:** ZestMoney

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** BNPL relies on high digital‑payment adoption and merchant integration, which are growing in India, but lower credit‑card penetration and stricter RBI regulations on short‑term credit could limit rapid scaling.
