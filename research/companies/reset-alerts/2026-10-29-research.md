# Reset Alerts

Researched: 2026-10-29

**Category:** password-management

**What it does:** Monitors password or credential expiration dates and sends SMS reminders to users so they can reset before access is lost. The service integrates with common authentication platforms and delivers alerts via text message.

**Likely revenue model:** Subscription SaaS for businesses, charging per user or per alert volume, with a free tier for limited alerts.

**India equivalent already exists:** none found

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The model relies on widespread SMS usage and a culture of proactive password rotation, which is less common in Indian enterprises that often use single sign‑on solutions without strict expiration policies. Additionally, telecom costs for bulk SMS could affect pricing viability.
