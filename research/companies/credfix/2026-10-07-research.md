# Credfix

Researched: 2026-10-07

**Category:** debt-tech

**What it does:** Credfix is an AI‑powered platform that helps borrowers assess their debt, explore repayment options, and interact with lenders through tools like an AI financial assistant, call‑shield, and legal assistant. It covers the entire debt‑resolution lifecycle from early distress signals to structured rehabilitation.

**Likely revenue model:** Charges a success‑based fee or commission on recovered or restructured debt, and may also offer subscription or SaaS fees to lenders for its AI tools.

**India equivalent already exists:** FREED

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The model relies on borrowers being willing to engage digitally with AI assistants and on lenders adopting tech‑enabled collection workflows, both of which are increasingly common in urban and tier‑2/3 India but may still face adoption lag in more cash‑centric segments.
