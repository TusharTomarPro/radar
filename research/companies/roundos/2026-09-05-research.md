# RoundOS

Researched: 2026-09-05

**Category:** data-room

**What it does:** RoundOS is a free alternative to Docsend, providing a virtual data room to securely share documents and track who views them.

**Likely revenue model:** Likely monetizes via paid tiers (advanced analytics, custom branding, larger storage) or per-seat/data-room fees for business use cases, with the free tier acting as a funnel.

**India equivalent already exists:** Docsend India alternatives like Digify and SmartRoom are present; additionally Paperflite operates in the same document-sharing/tracking space.

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** Indian startups and SMEs increasingly need lightweight data rooms for investor updates and sales collateral, but willingness to pay for premium analytics is low; adoption hinges on integrating with WhatsApp/email workflows common in India.
