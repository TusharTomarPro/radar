# Green Mountain Power

Researched: 2026-09-30

**Category:** virtual-power-plant

**What it does:** Aggregates residential battery storage into a virtual power plant that can supply electricity to the grid during outages or peak demand, reducing reliance on traditional power plants.

**Likely revenue model:** The utility earns revenue by charging customers a subscription or participation fee and by selling the aggregated capacity and ancillary services to the grid operator.

**India equivalent already exists:** Tata Power

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The model relies on widespread adoption of home battery systems and a regulatory framework that allows utilities to monetize distributed storage, both of which are still emerging in India, though pilot VPP projects are underway.
