# iChat

Researched: 2026-10-06

**Category:** video-conferencing

**What it does:** Revives Apple's legacy iChat service, adding modern audio and video calling capabilities for macOS users. It provides a lightweight, native client that integrates with existing Apple IDs.

**Likely revenue model:** Probably a freemium model with optional paid features like higher‑resolution streams, recording, or enterprise licensing.

**India equivalent already exists:** JioMeet

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The product relies on widespread macOS adoption and deep integration with Apple ID, which is less common in India where Android dominates; however, the underlying demand for simple video chat exists, so a similar service could work if adapted to Android and local authentication.
