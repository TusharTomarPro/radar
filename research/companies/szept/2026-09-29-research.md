# Szept

Researched: 2026-09-29

**Category:** voice-CRM

**What it does:** Szept lets users record a voice message, automatically transcribes it, and forwards the audio and text to a designated agent for follow‑up.

**Likely revenue model:** Businesses pay a subscription (or per‑message) fee to access the platform and its integration features.

**India equivalent already exists:** Exotel

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The model relies on widespread smartphone usage and reliable internet for voice note capture and transcription, which are available in urban India but may be limited in rural areas where connectivity and digital CRM adoption are lower.
