# Kinetic Age

Researched: 2026-10-06

**Category:** healthtech

**What it does:** Kinetic Age offers a subscription‑based, coordinated care platform for adults over 40, bundling doctor consultations, physiotherapy, fitness training, nutrition, diagnostics and emotional‑wellbeing support. The service uses AI‑driven care protocols and a network of providers to track adherence and deliver regular interventions.

**Likely revenue model:** Monthly or annual subscription fees paid by members, possibly tiered by level of services included.

**India equivalent already exists:** Portea Medical

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The model relies on a willingness of middle‑aged and senior Indians to pay for bundled, preventive health services and on a robust home‑care provider network, which is growing but still limited in tier‑2/3 cities. Adoption may be slower outside urban metros where awareness of preventive care is lower.
