# Seldon

Researched: 2026-11-10

**Category:** ai-testing

**What it does:** Seldon provides AI‑driven QA agents that interact with your product exactly as real customers would, automatically generating functional and usability tests. The agents simulate user flows, detect bugs, and surface quality issues without manual scripting.
