# Flipkart Minutes

Researched: 2026-09-05

**Category:** quick-commerce

**What it does:** Flipkart's quick commerce vertical offering rapid delivery (within minutes) of groceries and essentials through a network of strategically located dark stores, competing directly with other quick commerce players in Indian urban markets.

**Likely revenue model:** Transaction-based revenue from product sales, delivery fees charged to customers, and commissions from third-party sellers listed on the platform.

**India equivalent already exists:** BlinkIt (Zomato), Swiggy Instamart

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** This is already an Indian product operating successfully. However, the quick commerce model depends on high-density urban areas with favorable unit economics—many tier-2/3 cities may lack the order density to support similar dark store networks profitably.
