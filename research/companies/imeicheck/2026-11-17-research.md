# IMEICheck

Researched: 2026-11-17

**Category:** device-verification

**What it does:** Provides an online service that lets users input a phone’s IMEI to see its activation, blacklist, carrier lock and ownership history.

**Likely revenue model:** Charges a per‑lookup fee or a subscription for bulk checks to retailers and resellers.
