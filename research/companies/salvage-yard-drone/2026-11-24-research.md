# Salvage Yard Drone

Researched: 2026-11-24

**Category:** drone-mapping

**What it does:** Uses autonomous drones to capture aerial imagery of vehicle salvage yards and generates detailed 3D maps and inventory data for parts identification.

**Likely revenue model:** Charges salvage yards a subscription or per‑mapping fee for access to the platform and data analytics.
