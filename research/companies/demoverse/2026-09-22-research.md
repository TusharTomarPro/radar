# Demoverse

Researched: 2026-09-22

**Category:** co-creation

**What it does:** Demoverse provides an AI‑powered platform where brands can launch product‑design campaigns and let consumer communities co‑create designs in real time. The tool blends AI‑generated concepts with human preferences to surface validated product ideas.

**Likely revenue model:** It likely earns money by charging brands campaign fees, taking a commission on any resulting sales, and selling paid participation or subscription tiers to the most active community members.
