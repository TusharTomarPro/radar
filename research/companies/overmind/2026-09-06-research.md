# Overmind

Researched: 2026-09-06

**Category:** productivity

**What it does:** Overmind is a SaaS platform that centralises tasks, notes and workflows, aiming to automate routine work so users spend less time managing the tool itself. It blends task management with AI‑driven suggestions to turn the tool into an active assistant rather than a passive checklist.

**Likely revenue model:** A monthly/annual subscription tier with tiered pricing based on team size and feature access.

**India equivalent already exists:** Zoho Projects

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The model relies on widespread SaaS adoption and reliable broadband, both of which are growing in India’s urban and tech‑savvy segments, so the concept is viable. However, price sensitivity and preference for bundled suites like Zoho may affect uptake.
