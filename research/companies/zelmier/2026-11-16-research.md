# Zelmier

Researched: 2026-11-16

**Category:** govtech

**What it does:** Zelmier aggregates government tenders from over 250 portals and uses AI to surface the most relevant opportunities for businesses, reducing the time to discover and bid on contracts to minutes.

**Likely revenue model:** A subscription‑based SaaS model where firms pay recurring fees for access to the AI‑driven tender feed, possibly supplemented by success‑based commissions on won contracts.

**India equivalent already exists:** TenderTiger

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** India already has a fragmented tender ecosystem and firms are accustomed to manually searching portals; the AI‑matching layer would work but requires reliable digital adoption and trust in automated recommendations, which may take time to gain traction.
