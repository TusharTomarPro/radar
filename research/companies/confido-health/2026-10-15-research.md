# Confido Health

Researched: 2026-10-15

**Category:** healthtech

**What it does:** Confido Health provides AI‑driven agents that automate healthcare office tasks such as appointment scheduling, insurance verification and care coordination, integrating with existing EHR and practice‑management systems via a no‑code setup. The platform triggers end‑to‑end workflows from a single patient interaction.

**Likely revenue model:** Subscription‑based SaaS licensing per practice or provider, possibly with usage‑based fees for high‑volume workflows.

**India equivalent already exists:** Practo

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** Indian clinics often rely on paper‑based processes and lack standardized EMR integration, so widespread adoption of a plug‑and‑play AI agent may be slower; however, larger private hospitals and chain clinics that already use digital practice‑management tools could adopt it more readily.
