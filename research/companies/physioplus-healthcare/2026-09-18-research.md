# Physioplus Healthcare

Researched: 2026-09-18

**Category:** healthtech

**What it does:** Physioplus Healthcare is a digital platform that connects patients with certified physiotherapists for remote consultations, assessments, and monitored rehabilitation. It offers practice management tools, clinical documentation, and technology‑enabled monitoring for chronic pain, sports injuries and post‑surgery care.

**Likely revenue model:** The startup likely earns revenue through a commission on each physiotherapy session booked and subscription fees paid by physiotherapists for access to the practice‑management suite.

**India equivalent already exists:** PhysioConnect

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The model relies on widespread broadband access and a cultural shift toward accepting remote physiotherapy, which is strong in urban India but less prevalent in rural areas. It also depends on a sufficient pool of digitally‑savvy physiotherapists willing to adopt tele‑care.
