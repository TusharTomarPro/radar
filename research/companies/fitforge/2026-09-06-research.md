# FitForge

Researched: 2026-09-06

**Category:** fitness-tech

**What it does:** FitForge offers a SaaS platform for fitness coaches and gyms to manage client communications, workout plans, and progress tracking, with a free tier to attract users.

**Likely revenue model:** Freemium model – free tier for basic messaging and limited features, with paid subscription plans for advanced tools and larger client bases.

**India equivalent already exists:** Fittr

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The model relies on coaches adopting a digital client‑management workflow and users being comfortable with online messaging, which are growing but still uneven across smaller towns in India; payment infrastructure is sufficient for subscription fees.
