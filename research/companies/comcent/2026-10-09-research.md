# Comcent

Researched: 2026-10-09

**Category:** voice-infra

**What it does:** Provides an AI‑ready voice infrastructure that sits on a company’s own SIP trunk, enabling developers to add AI capabilities (e.g., transcription, sentiment analysis, bots) to inbound and outbound calls.

**Likely revenue model:** Charges a subscription fee per SIP trunk or a usage‑based fee for AI processing minutes/API calls.

**India equivalent already exists:** Exotel

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The model relies on enterprises already having SIP trunk connectivity and being comfortable exposing call streams to AI services; while SIP trunking is common in India, data‑privacy concerns and telecom regulations could slow adoption of AI‑driven voice layers.
