# Nudgebox

Researched: 2026-10-16

**Category:** web-push

**What it does:** Nudgebox lets businesses send browser push notifications to users without having to develop a native mobile app, using a simple integration snippet. It provides a dashboard to create, schedule, and target messages.

**Likely revenue model:** Subscription SaaS with tiered plans based on monthly notification volume and advanced targeting features.

**India equivalent already exists:** MoEngage

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** India has high mobile and internet usage, but browser‑based push adoption is lower than app notifications, especially on iOS where support is limited. The model works if users are accustomed to opting in to web push, which may require education and strong value propositions.

**Badge guess:** orange
