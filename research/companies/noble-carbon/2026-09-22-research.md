# Noble Carbon

Researched: 2026-09-22

**Category:** ev-charging

**What it does:** Offers a smart circuit breaker that lets homeowners add EV chargers without needing a costly main panel upgrade.

**Likely revenue model:** Sells the smart breaker hardware (and possibly a monitoring subscription) directly to consumers or through installers.

**India equivalent already exists:** Schneider Electric India

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** Indian homes often have limited panel capacity and EV penetration is still low, so while the technology is feasible, adoption may be slowed by cost sensitivity and the need for consumer awareness about EV charging infrastructure.
