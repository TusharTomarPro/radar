# Sellio

Researched: 2026-10-04

**Category:** ai-support

**What it does:** Sellio offers a shared inbox for customer support that is powered by AI agents which can automatically respond to and triage tickets.

**Likely revenue model:** A subscription SaaS model charging businesses per seat or per volume of tickets processed.

**India equivalent already exists:** Freshworks (Freshdesk)

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The model relies on widespread internet access and willingness of Indian SMBs to adopt AI chatbots; while infrastructure exists, multilingual support and trust in AI responses could be hurdles.
