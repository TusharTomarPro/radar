# iSurge

Researched: 2026-10-30

**Category:** wearables

**What it does:** iSurge is a compact activity monitor that attaches to the notch of a smartphone, tracking metrics like steps, heart rate, and movement without a separate wristband.

**Likely revenue model:** Revenue likely comes from one‑time hardware sales plus optional subscription fees for advanced analytics and personalized coaching.

**India equivalent already exists:** GOQii

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The product assumes users have phones with a prominent notch and are comfortable wearing a small sensor on the device, which may be less common in India where many users prefer wrist‑worn wearables; however, the overall demand for health tracking is strong.
