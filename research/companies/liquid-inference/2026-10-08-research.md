# Liquid Inference

Researched: 2026-10-08

**Category:** ai-marketplace

**What it does:** Liquid Inference is a routing platform that forwards each LLM prompt to multiple AI providers, letting them compete to deliver the best answer. It selects the optimal response and abstracts the provider layer for developers.

**Likely revenue model:** Charges a transaction fee or commission on each routed request, possibly with a subscription tier for higher volume or premium routing features.

**India equivalent already exists:** none found

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The model relies on a dense ecosystem of competing LLM providers and low‑latency internet connectivity, which are less mature in India; enterprises may also be wary of sending proprietary data to multiple external AI services.
