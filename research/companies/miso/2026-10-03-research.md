# Miso

Researched: 2026-10-03

**Category:** travel-booking

**What it does:** Miso lets users search for and book flights and hotels directly within the iMessage app, turning a chat conversation into a booking interface.

**Likely revenue model:** Earns commissions or referral fees from airlines and hotels for each booking made through the platform, possibly supplemented by a service fee to the user.

**India equivalent already exists:** MakeMyTrip

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** Miso relies on iMessage, which has low penetration in India where Android and WhatsApp dominate; the chat‑based booking experience may not translate well without a comparable messaging ecosystem.

**Badge guess:** orange
