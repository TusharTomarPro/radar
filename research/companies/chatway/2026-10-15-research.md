# Chatway

Researched: 2026-10-15

**Category:** ai-support

**What it does:** Chatway integrates LLMs such as ChatGPT and Claude into a message‑center platform to automatically draft and send replies to customer support tickets.

**Likely revenue model:** Subscription SaaS pricing per seat or per ticket volume, possibly with tiered plans for larger teams.

**India equivalent already exists:** Freshworks (Freshdesk)
