# TinyKPI

Researched: 2026-09-17

**Category:** business-analytics

**What it does:** TinyKPI delivers key business metrics as a tiny overlay that lives in the phone’s notch or a similar small UI space, letting users glance at live KPI data without opening a full dashboard.

**Likely revenue model:** A subscription‑based SaaS model with tiered plans based on number of metrics, users, and data source integrations.

**India equivalent already exists:** Zoho Analytics

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The concept relies on users constantly checking a small screen area, which works well with high smartphone penetration in India, but may be limited by older devices lacking a notch or by user habits that favor full‑screen apps.
