# TinyKPI

Researched: 2026-09-17

**Category:** business-analytics

**What it does:** TinyKPI delivers key business metrics as a tiny overlay that lives in the phone’s notch or a similar small UI space, letting users glance at live KPI data without opening a full dashboard.
