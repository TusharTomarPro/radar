# Mira

Researched: 2026-11-24

**Category:** smart-tv

**What it does:** Mira offers a compact HDMI dongle that adds streaming apps, voice control and a universal remote interface to any traditional TV, turning it into a basic smart TV.

**Likely revenue model:** Revenue comes from selling the hardware device outright, with optional subscription fees for premium content or advanced voice‑assistant features.

**India equivalent already exists:** Amazon Fire TV Stick

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The concept works in India as most homes have HDMI‑compatible TVs and broadband, but price sensitivity and strong competition from low‑cost local dongles could hinder widespread adoption.
