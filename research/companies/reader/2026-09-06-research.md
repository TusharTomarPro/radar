# Reader

Researched: 2026-09-06

**Category:** news-aggregator

**What it does:** Offers a single subscription that unlocks a curated library of premium publications, letting readers access multiple paywalled sites without separate logins.

**Likely revenue model:** Charges users a monthly fee and distributes a portion of that revenue to the partner publishers.
