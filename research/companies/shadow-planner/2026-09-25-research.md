# Shadow Planner

Researched: 2026-09-25

**Category:** project-management

**What it does:** Shadow Planner uses AI to automatically generate Gantt charts and project timelines based on user inputs, running entirely on the user's device. It helps teams visualize and adjust schedules without manual charting.
