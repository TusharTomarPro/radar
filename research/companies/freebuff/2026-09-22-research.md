# Freebuff

Researched: 2026-09-22

**Category:** dev-adtech

**What it does:** Freebuff offers advertising slots within its AI-powered coding agent, letting brands reach a community of about 500,000 developers directly in their development workflow.

**Likely revenue model:** Charges advertisers on a CPM/CPC basis for ad impressions or clicks delivered through the coding agent.

**India equivalent already exists:** GeeksforGeeks

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** India has a large developer base, but widespread adoption of AI coding assistants is still emerging, and developers may be resistant to in‑tool ads, which could limit effectiveness compared to more established platforms like GeeksforGeeks.
