# Crematorium Heat

Researched: 2026-10-30

**Category:** waste-heat

**What it does:** Captures excess thermal energy from crematoria and channels it into nearby district‑heating networks to warm homes and swimming pools.

**Likely revenue model:** Charges municipalities or property owners for the delivered heat on a per‑kilowatt‑hour basis, often bundled with carbon‑credit incentives.
