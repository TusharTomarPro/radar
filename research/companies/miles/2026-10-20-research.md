# Miles

Researched: 2026-10-20

**Category:** auto-telematics

**What it does:** The Miles Plug is an OBD‑II device that tracks a car’s location, driving habits and health, and can automatically summon emergency assistance and roadside support after a crash.

**Likely revenue model:** They sell the plug for a one‑time price and charge a monthly or annual subscription for the emergency response and roadside‑assistance services.

**India equivalent already exists:** LocoNav

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** India’s vehicle fleet is dominated by two‑wheelers and older cars without standardized OBD‑II ports, limiting adoption; plus the country’s emergency response network is less integrated, which could reduce the plug’s value proposition.
