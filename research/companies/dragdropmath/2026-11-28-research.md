# DragDropMath

Researched: 2026-11-28

**Category:** edtech

**What it does:** DragDropMath is a web‑based tool that teaches users to simplify algebraic expressions and solve equations by dragging symbols, allowing only mathematically correct moves. Teachers can generate custom problem sets that are shared via URL links.

**Likely revenue model:** Probably a freemium model where basic puzzles are free and schools or teachers pay a subscription for premium content, analytics, and custom problem‑set creation.

**India equivalent already exists:** BYJU'S
