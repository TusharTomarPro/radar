# GPUComputePay

Researched: 2026-09-13

**Category:** ai-agent

**What it does:** Provides a platform where autonomous AI agents can request GPU compute and automatically pay for it in real‑time using USDC cryptocurrency. The system settles the transaction instantly as the compute is consumed.
