# Trigger.dev

Researched: 2026-10-28

**Category:** ai-agent

**What it does:** Trigger.dev provides webhook infrastructure that lets developers connect AI agents to over 50 services such as Slack and Supabase, enabling automated workflows. It abstracts the plumbing so agents can be triggered and respond via familiar APIs.

**Likely revenue model:** A tiered SaaS subscription model with usage‑based pricing for higher webhook volumes and premium features.

**India equivalent already exists:** Kissflow

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The model relies on widespread developer adoption of AI agents and reliable internet/webhook connectivity, which are available in India, but enterprise willingness to expose internal systems to external webhook platforms may be slower.
