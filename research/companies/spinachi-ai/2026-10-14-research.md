# Spinachi AI

Researched: 2026-10-14

**Category:** health-tech

**What it does:** Spinachi AI provides a family‑wide personal health manager that interacts via WhatsApp, offering health tracking, medication reminders, symptom triage and basic medical advice. It leverages AI to interpret user inputs and coordinate care across family members.

**Likely revenue model:** A subscription‑based model per household (or per user) with optional premium features such as doctor consultations or integration with labs.
