# Sendoff

Researched: 2026-11-07

**Category:** email-automation

**What it does:** Sendoff lets users trigger an email blast to their subscriber list with a simple voice command like “send it.” It connects to existing email list providers and automates the dispatch without manual setup.

**Likely revenue model:** Subscription‑based SaaS with tiered plans (free tier for limited sends, paid tiers for higher volume and advanced features).

**India equivalent already exists:** Zoho Campaigns

**India fit note (AI first pass, verify yourself):** The core functionality—automated email blasts—is well‑matched to India's mature email‑marketing ecosystem, but reliance on voice‑command triggers may face slower adoption due to lower penetration of voice assistants in professional settings.
