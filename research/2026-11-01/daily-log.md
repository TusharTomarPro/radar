## BBRouter
- Source article: [Show HN: BBRouter – Ben Eater's 8-bit computer, simulated on 3D breadboards](https://bbrouter.com/bbrouter/ben-eater-sap-1) (Hacker News (front page))
- Category: hardware-simulation
- What it does: A web‑based virtual breadboard editor that lets users design, simulate, and debug Ben Eater‑style 8‑bit computers with features like side‑by‑side diff, oscilloscope view, auto‑router and AI‑assisted troubleshooting.
- Likely revenue model: Subscription‑based SaaS with a monthly Maker plan (≈$5‑$7 per month) after a free trial.
- India equivalent: CircuitVerse
- India fit note: The product relies on high‑speed broadband and a subscription mindset, which are growing in India’s urban maker community, but limited internet bandwidth and lower willingness to pay for niche tools could hinder adoption.
- Badge guess: orange

- Category: DIY hardware
