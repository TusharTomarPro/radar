- What it does: Provides an open‑source, self‑hosted platform that combines ERP, MES and QMS capabilities for manufacturers to manage production, inventory, quality and compliance.

## ByteAsk

## Canine Cancer Detection
- Source article: [Sniffing dogs join the race to catch cancer early in India](https://the-ken.com/story/sniffing-dogs-join-the-race-to-catch-cancer-early-in-india/) (The Ken (public feed))
- Category: health-tech
- What it does: Trains specially selected dogs to sniff biological samples (breath, urine, blood) and flag early‑stage cancers with high accuracy. The service is offered to hospitals and screening centers for non‑invasive early detection.
- Likely revenue model: Charges a per‑test fee to hospitals/clinics or a subscription for a set number of screenings per month.
- India equivalent: none found
- India fit note: Success depends on regulatory clearance for animal‑based diagnostics, acceptance by doctors and patients, and a reliable supply chain for trained detection dogs—factors that are still nascent in India.
- Badge guess: black
