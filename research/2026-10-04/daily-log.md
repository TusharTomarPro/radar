## BinRange
- Source article: [Surely you have ultra-wideband radios on your bins too?](https://sjg.io/writing/binrange-have-you-actually-put-the-bins-out/) (Hacker News (front page))
- Category: waste-tech
- What it does: BinRange equips municipal waste bins with ultra‑wideband (UWB) radios that broadcast precise real‑time location data, allowing cities to optimise collection routes and reduce missed pickups.
- Likely revenue model: They sell the UWB sensor hardware to municipalities and charge a recurring subscription for data analytics, routing software, and ongoing support.
- India equivalent: SmartBin (SmartBin Technologies)
- India fit note: The solution relies on high‑precision UWB positioning and a centralized data platform, which may be costly and require municipalities to adopt formal route‑planning processes that are less common in India's largely informal waste‑collection ecosystem.
- Badge guess: orange

## Liao AI
- Source article: [Agents don't need memory, they need documentation](https://liao.gg/blog/agents-dont-need-memory) (Hacker News (front page))
- Category: ai-agent
- What it does: Offers a framework that lets autonomous AI agents query external documentation in real time instead of relying on internal memory, keeping responses current and accurate.
- Likely revenue model: Charges enterprises a subscription fee for API access and usage‑based pricing for documentation‑query calls.
- India equivalent: none found
- India fit note: The model assumes widespread, low‑latency access to structured internal knowledge bases and a culture of integrating AI agents into workflows, which many Indian firms are still adopting.
- Badge guess: black
