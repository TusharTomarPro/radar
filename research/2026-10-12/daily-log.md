## Archive Hunt
- Source article: [So, You’d Like To Sell Your Print Issues Of The Onion?](https://theonion.com/archivehunt/) (Hacker News (front page))
- Category: collectibles marketplace
- What it does: Runs an online platform where users can list, buy, and sell vintage print issues of The Onion newspaper.
- Likely revenue model: Charges a commission fee on each sale (and possibly optional listing fees).
- India equivalent: eBay India
- India fit note: The model relies on a niche collector culture and reliable cross‑border shipping; while e‑commerce logistics exist in India, demand for vintage US satirical newspapers may be low, and customs/shipping costs could hinder adoption.
- Badge guess: orange
