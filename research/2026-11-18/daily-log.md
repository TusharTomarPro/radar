## Sqwish Labs
- Source article: [As decision model use skyrockets, is customization the next frontier?](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/as-decision-model-use-skyrockets-is-customization-is-the-next-frontier/) (TechCrunch)
- Category: ai-platform
- What it does: Sqwish Labs offers a decision‑model service that can be fine‑tuned with a user’s proprietary data, delivering more accurate, domain‑specific predictions.
- Likely revenue model: A SaaS subscription with tiered pricing based on data volume, model training frequency, and API call limits.
- India equivalent: Arya.ai
- India fit note: Custom model training requires large, clean datasets and reliable cloud compute; Indian firms often face data‑privacy constraints and variable cloud bandwidth, which could slow adoption outside data‑rich sectors like fintech or e‑commerce.
- Badge guess: orange

## PostHog Decision Models
- Source article: [Not Everything Needs an LLM](https://newsletter.posthog.com/p/decision-models) (Hacker News (front page))
- Category: product-analytics
- What it does: Provides a no‑code decision engine that lets product teams define rule‑based actions and automations on user events without using LLMs. Teams can trigger notifications, feature flags, or workflows based on real‑time behavior.
- Likely revenue model: SaaS subscription with tiered pricing based on event volume and feature access.
- India equivalent: MoEngage
- India fit note: The solution relies on mature event‑tracking and real‑time data pipelines, which many Indian SaaS firms have, but smaller startups may lack the analytics maturity or data‑privacy frameworks needed for widespread adoption.
- Badge guess: orange
