## Green Mountain Power
- Source article: [Vermont replacing power plants with home batteries](https://www.bbc.com/future/article/20260928-a-virtual-power-plant-hidden-in-vermont-homes-is-keeping-the-lights-on-during-storms) (Hacker News (front page))
- Category: virtual-power-plant
- What it does: Aggregates residential battery storage into a virtual power plant that can supply electricity to the grid during outages or peak demand, reducing reliance on traditional power plants.
- Likely revenue model: The utility earns revenue by charging customers a subscription or participation fee and by selling the aggregated capacity and ancillary services to the grid operator.
- India equivalent: Tata Power
- India fit note: The model relies on widespread adoption of home battery systems and a regulatory framework that allows utilities to monetize distributed storage, both of which are still emerging in India, though pilot VPP projects are underway.
- Badge guess: orange

## NSL
- Source article: [Show HN: NSL – WSL for Linux](https://frostyard.github.io/nsl/) (Hacker News (front page))
- Category: dev-tools
- What it does: Provides a WSL‑like development environment on Linux by running a single VM that hosts systemd‑nspawn containers for each distro, handling host file edits and port sharing.
- Likely revenue model: Open‑source project likely monetized via donations, sponsorships, or paid support services.
- India equivalent: none found
- India fit note: The solution depends on users having a Linux host with virtualization support and familiarity with container tooling; while Linux adoption is rising in India, many developers still use Windows/macOS and corporate policies may restrict VM usage, limiting immediate traction.
- Badge guess: black
