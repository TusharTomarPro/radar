## Lend
- Source article: [Running coding agents without handing them my credentials](https://blog.antonio.cx/running-agents-with-lend) (Hacker News (front page))
- Category: ai-agent
- What it does: Lend provides a sandboxed execution layer that lets AI coding agents run code and perform tasks without being given the user's actual credentials, using temporary, revocable tokens.
- Likely revenue model: Subscription or usage‑based pricing for access to the secure execution platform and API calls.
- India equivalent: none found
- India fit note: The model relies on widespread cloud infrastructure, fine‑grained IAM token services, and a developer culture comfortable with delegating temporary credentials—capabilities that are growing in India but may still be limited in smaller firms.
- Badge guess: black

## Handpickd
- Source article: [Handpickd posts Rs 28.6 Cr revenue and Rs 43 Cr loss in FY26](https://entrackr.com/fintrackr/handpickd-posts-rs-286-cr-revenue-and-rs-43-cr-loss-in-fy26-12815932) (Entrackr)
- Category: agri-commerce
- What it does: Handpickd runs an inventory‑free farm‑to‑fresh delivery service that collects customer orders, sources the exact quantities from farmers, and delivers the produce the next morning. It aims to minimise waste by avoiding warehousing and dark‑store stock.
- Likely revenue model: The company earns revenue by selling farm produce directly to consumers at a markup and also pockets interest income from customer deposits.
- India equivalent: Milkbasket
- India fit note: The model relies on a highly responsive supply chain, reliable cold‑chain logistics and consumers willing to pay a premium for next‑day fresh produce—capabilities that are uneven across Indian tier‑2 and tier‑3 cities. Scaling such on‑demand procurement also demands strong farmer onboarding platforms that are still nascent in many regions.
- Badge guess: orange
