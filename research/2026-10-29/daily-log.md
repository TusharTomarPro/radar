## Readwise
- Source article: [Readwise is taking on Kindle and Kobo for ebook supremacy](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1020264/readwise-books-launch-ebook-store) (The Verge)
- Category: reading-app
- What it does: Readwise aggregates and categorizes highlights from Kindle, Kobo and other ebook sources, and offers a unified reader for newsletters, PDFs and web articles. The new Readwise Books product adds a full‑featured ebook library and reading experience.
- Likely revenue model: Subscription‑based premium plans (monthly/annual) with a free tier that limits syncs and features.
- India equivalent: none found
- India fit note: The service relies heavily on Kindle/Kobo ecosystems and on users habitually exporting highlights, which are less common in India where Kindle penetration is low and readers often use native apps or PDFs. Subscription willingness for niche reading tools may also be limited.
