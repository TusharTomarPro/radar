## FlattenSF
- Source article: [Find the flattest route between any two points in SF](https://flattensf.com/) (Hacker News (front page))
- Category: mobility
- What it does: Provides a route‑planning service that finds the flattest walking or cycling path between any two points in San Francisco, minimizing elevation gain. Users can view elevation profiles and choose routes optimized for low incline.
- Likely revenue model: Offers a free basic service and monetizes through a premium subscription for advanced features, API access for developers, and possibly local advertising or partnership deals.
- India equivalent: MapmyIndia
- India fit note: While MapmyIndia offers route planning, Indian cities have less demand for flat‑only routes due to lower cycling/walking culture and inconsistent sidewalk infrastructure. Additionally, elevation data quality varies, which could limit the usefulness of a purely flat‑route service.
- Badge guess: orange
