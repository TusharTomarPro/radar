## rqlite
- Source article: [Twelve Years of Distributed SQLite](https://philipotoole.com/twelve-years-of-distributed-sqlite/) (Hacker News (front page))
- Category: distributed-db
- What it does: Provides a fault‑tolerant, distributed version of SQLite using the Raft consensus algorithm, allowing multiple nodes to share a single relational database.
- Likely revenue model: Offers paid support, consulting, and hosted managed service subscriptions for enterprises.
- India equivalent: none found
- India fit note: Requires reliable low‑latency network and persistent storage across nodes, which may be limited in many Indian edge deployments, and adoption depends on familiarity with SQLite over more common NoSQL solutions.
- Badge guess: black

## Hotshare
- Source article: [Rs 10,000 Cr into restaurants. But why do 80% fail? Here's the Hotshare F&B investment playbook](https://yourstory.com/2026/10/the-hotshare-fb-investment-playbook) (YourStory)
- Category: restaurant-investment
- What it does: Hotshare is an F&B investment platform that sources, evaluates, and funds restaurant startups, providing capital and strategic support.
- Likely revenue model: It takes equity stakes in the restaurants it backs and earns returns through exits, dividends, or profit‑sharing arrangements.
- India equivalent: Zomato Capital
- India fit note: The model relies on a mature venture‑capital ecosystem and a large pool of restaurant entrepreneurs, which exist in India, but success still hinges on consumer dining habits and high operating costs that can differ regionally.
- Badge guess: orange
