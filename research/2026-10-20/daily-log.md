## Miles
- Source article: [I tried a $99 plug that promises to track your car and send help when needed — without selling your data](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1013918/miles-plug-vehicle-tracking-data-privacy-specs-price-launch-date) (The Verge)
- Category: auto-telematics
- What it does: The Miles Plug is an OBD‑II device that tracks a car’s location, driving habits and health, and can automatically summon emergency assistance and roadside support after a crash.
- Likely revenue model: They sell the plug for a one‑time price and charge a monthly or annual subscription for the emergency response and roadside‑assistance services.
- India equivalent: LocoNav
- India fit note: India’s vehicle fleet is dominated by two‑wheelers and older cars without standardized OBD‑II ports, limiting adoption; plus the country’s emergency response network is less integrated, which could reduce the plug’s value proposition.
- Badge guess: orange
