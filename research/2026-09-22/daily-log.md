## Spymarks
- Source article: [Spymarks, Not Watermarks](https://brand.io/article/spymarks/) (Hacker News (front page))
- Category: brand-protection
- What it does: Spymarks embeds invisible, tamper‑proof markers into digital assets (images, videos, PDFs) that let owners trace where the content appears online and detect unauthorized use. The system provides real‑time alerts and forensic proof for takedown or legal action.
- Likely revenue model: A SaaS subscription tiered by the number of assets monitored and alerts generated, with optional per‑incident enforcement fees.
- India equivalent: none found
- India fit note: The model relies on widespread adoption of invisible watermarking standards and a legal ecosystem that can act quickly on infringement notices, both of which are less mature in India, limiting immediate traction.
- Badge guess: black

## Draftable
- Source article: [I don't want to read what you didn't write](https://blog.colinbreck.com/i-dont-want-to-read-what-you-didnt-write/) (Hacker News (front page))
- Category: document-comparison
- What it does: Draftable provides a SaaS platform that lets users upload two versions of a document and instantly see only the changes highlighted, removing the need to read unchanged text.
- Likely revenue model: They charge a subscription fee per user or per seat, with tiered pricing for higher volume and API access.
- India equivalent: Zoho Docs (document compare feature)
- India fit note: The service relies on widespread digital document workflows and cloud storage, which are common in Indian enterprises but less prevalent among small, paper‑based businesses, potentially limiting adoption outside larger firms.
- Badge guess: orange
