## CorridorKey
- Source article: [CorridorKey 2.0](https://corridorkey.com/) (Hacker News (front page))
- Category: devsecops
- What it does: CorridorKey 2.0 is a SaaS platform that lets teams securely store, share, and rotate SSH keys, passwords and other secrets with audit trails and automated expiry.
- Likely revenue model: Subscription‑based pricing per user or per secret, with tiered plans for enterprises.
- India equivalent: ManageEngine Password Manager Pro (Zoho)
- India fit note: The product assumes widespread cloud adoption and a culture of remote/DevOps‑centric workflows, which are growing in India but still face legacy on‑prem security policies in many enterprises.
