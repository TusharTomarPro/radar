## Is It Greg?
- Source article: [Show HN: Is It Greg?](https://github.com/antoineleclair/is-it-greg) (Hacker News (front page))
- Category: browser-extension
- What it does: A Chrome extension that scans Hacker News (and other sites) for links or mentions of the developer Greg and highlights them so users never miss his creations. It flags URLs pointing to greg.technology or its subdomains.
- Likely revenue model: Probably offered for free, monetized via donations, sponsorships, or a future premium version with extra filtering features.
- India equivalent: none found
- India fit note: The product relies on a niche community of developers tracking a single individual's output, which may not have a sizable user base in India. It also assumes widespread Chrome usage and familiarity with browser extensions, which are common but the specific use‑case is unlikely to gain traction.
- Badge guess: black

## PredictionSocial
- Source article: [Show HN: Exploring the intersection of prediction markets and social media](https://www.thevidmarket.com/) (Hacker News (front page))
- Category: prediction-market
- What it does: Merges social media activity with prediction market contracts, allowing users to wager on the future popularity of topics or content.
- Likely revenue model: Charges a transaction fee on each bet and offers premium analytics or subscription tiers for advanced users.
- India equivalent: none found
- India fit note: India lacks clear regulations for prediction markets and betting platforms, which could limit legal operation despite high social media engagement.
- Badge guess: black
